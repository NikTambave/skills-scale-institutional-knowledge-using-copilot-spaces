# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme's centralized project management knowledge hub. This folder contains comprehensive guidance on how we plan, execute, and deliver projects effectively.

## Quick Start

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named PM and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## OctoAcme Project Management Overview

OctoAcme's project management approach is a structured lifecycle that moves from initiation to planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. In the initiation phase, teams validate the business need, identify stakeholders, define a one-pager with goals and success metrics, and decide whether the project should move forward into planning. Planning then turns the initiative into an actionable backlog with milestones, dependencies, and a clear definition of done. Execution follows a team rhythm that includes daily standups, weekly delivery reviews, sprint or milestone demos, and explicit tracking in project boards or GitHub Projects to help teams monitor progress and blockers. Release and retrospective activities ensure that work ends with verification, stakeholder communication, and learning captured for future improvements.

The documentation emphasizes clear ownership and role clarity across the project. Product leads define outcomes and prioritize value, project managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communications, developers build and test the solution, and QA/testing ensures features meet acceptance criteria. Stakeholders provide input and approvals, while cross-functional coordination helps manage trade-offs and dependencies. This persona-driven structure makes responsibilities explicit and helps teams align around customer value, delivery timelines, and consistent status reporting. The project framework also highlights the value of a project charter, risk register, roadmap, backlog, and retrospective notes as the main artifacts that keep work transparent and repeatable.

Communication is treated as a core discipline rather than an afterthought. OctoAcme recommends a regular cadence of weekly PM/PdM updates, twice-weekly delivery standups, monthly stakeholder reporting, and ad-hoc escalations when risks or blockers demand intervention. The documentation also defines escalation paths for issues, from team triage to PM, product lead, and sponsor involvement for business-impacting problems, along with specific guidance for incident communication and security escalation. This ensures stakeholders receive consistent, single-source updates and that blockers are surfaced early enough to reduce disruption. Quality assurance is embedded throughout the workflow and is intended to reduce risk before release, with emphasis on test coverage, CI checks, security scanning, smoke tests, and manual QA when needed.

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a structured five-phase approach:

### 1. [Project Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md)
Validate business need, identify stakeholders, create a lightweight plan, and make the go/no-go decision.

### 2. [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md)
Break work into shippable increments, identify dependencies and risks, align timelines and responsibilities.

### 3. [Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)
Manage day-to-day execution, track progress toward milestones, maintain quality standards, and escalate blockers.

### 4. [Release & Deployment](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md)
Standardize how we release features to production, reduce risk, and improve observability.

### 5. [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)
Capture learnings after sprints, releases, or milestones and convert them into actionable improvements.
